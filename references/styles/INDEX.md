# Style index

53 styles: 36 built treatments and 17 research recipes. Each name links to its card; mockups are rendered screens of the same sample app in that style.

## How to read this

- **Status:** *Built ★★★* is recognisable at a glance and usable as is; *★★* is recognisable with known weaknesses (see the card's Source line); *★* needs a redo. A *research recipe* is a sourced hypothesis with no tested treatment. Don't present it as proven.
- **Layer:** a *Material* style is a way of making surfaces (brushwork, paper, dots, light) that works on any subject, so it suits real products. A *period specimen* is strongest as tribute, campaign or easter egg; carry its language over carefully (see `../translating-a-style.md`).
- **Web fit:** the card's first-line verdict. Read the card for where it fits and where it doesn't.
- **Picking for a product UI:** start with built material styles rated High. For a campaign page or easter egg, anything goes, but say what's unproven.
